What we roast

Whole bean, roasted in small batches

Every order is roasted fresh, never pulled from a warehouse shelf. Pick what suits your morning, your mug, or your mood.

Single Origin

One place, one harvest, one story in the cup. We source from quality importers who support indigenous coffee growers around the world.

Specialty Blends

Blends we build with care, balancing sweetness, body and brightness so they taste great from the first cup to the last.

Decaf Done Right

Great decaf shouldn't feel like a consolation prize. Ours is roasted with the same care as everything else, so you don't feel like you're missing out.

Your roast, your rules

Tell us how you like it and we'll roast to your preference. Caffeinated or decaf, every style is on the menu. We've got caffeine minds covered, and decaf minds too.

Made for you

Whimsical, personalized labels

Every bag gets a label with some personality. Name it after your cabin, your café, your best friend, or your terrible dog. Giving a gift? Put a joke on it. Hosting an event? Make it a party favor.

Each label also tells the coffee's story: the roast date, the origin, and the elevation it was grown at, when we have it.

Coffee should be fun, and a bag with your name on it is more fun than a plain one.

Our story

Roots in Italy, roasted with love

We trace our roots to the Italian peninsula, where coffee is a national pastime and gathering around food and drink is as natural as breathing. That spirit is baked into every roast.

We're engineers, and we love the science of coffee from root and stem to bean and roast. We take our roasting very seriously. We just don't take ourselves too seriously.
